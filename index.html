Get to know us

Dreams are better
when comfort comes first.

At Comfort Sleep, we are dedicated to crafting dreams by delivering exceptional sleep solutions.

Established in August 2017 in the serene city of Mysuru, Karnataka, our journey has been marked by innovation, quality and a passion for enhancing your rest.

With ISO 9001:2015 certification, we offer a diverse portfolio of over 25 meticulously designed mattress brands. Our range features materials and constructions selected to suit different comfort and support preferences.

We go beyond industry standards by emphasizing rigorous testing and durability. Our focus lies in combining thoughtful design with customer-centric service to provide targeted support where it matters most.
